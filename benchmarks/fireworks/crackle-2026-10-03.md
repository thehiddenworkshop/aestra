# F5C — bounded delayed-carrier crackle, 2026-10-03

An ordinary editable asset now represents three particle-driven generations:
rocket death → 96 main stars → 96 burning carriers → 12 short sparks per carrier death.
This validates delayed small-event behavior at bounded high-tier density, not AAA artistic
quality or finale throughput. No core runtime change or new firework-specific trigger.

## Asset and host contract

`assets/test/effects/fireworks_crackle.aestra.ron` uses seven emitters and five death links.
The rocket also feeds one flash and 48 smoke particles. Main stars live 0.75–1.05 seconds
at speed 18–22. A main death creates one carrier, inheriting 85% of parent velocity;
its speed is 0.8–1.8 and seeded lifetime 0.12–0.35 seconds. Carrier death creates 12 sparks,
inheriting 15% velocity plus spherical speed 4–8. Their lifetime 0.08–0.20 seconds, brief
opacity/size decay and white-to-gold cooling make short pops. Carriers and pops have no trails.
Existing launch/main histories, exposed color/radiance parameters and hero HDR materials
are reused. Total particle capacity is 1,458. Smoke remains an unlit approximation.

The fixture exports `launch`, `main_break`, and `crackle`. The last comes from real carrier
deaths, not a timer or animation curve. `FirstPerTick` coalesces representative effect-local
position and carrier count. The host maps it to `host/firework_crackle`; actual audio assets,
voice pooling, placement and mixing remain host-owned. F5B's 32-tick lossy readback ring and
qualified epoch policy apply unchanged. Event-created births deliberately do not raise
`OnSpawn` in the current contract; spark admission is measured by link counters, and the
conformance test independently verifies their live positions and eventual death outputs.

## Native evidence

Reports are local ignored artifacts under `target/fireworks-f5/`. Hardware: RTX 4070 SUPER /
Vulkan, dev build, 960×540, high tier, seed `0xf1e0000000000001`, playback-only, manual 60 Hz,
semantic materials, HDR/Tony/exposure 0/bloom 0.15, authored pixel floors 0/0.

- `f5c-crackle-live.json`: uninterrupted live simulation, 120 warm-up / 600 measured frames,
  stable ordering. All five links report demand = accepted **96 / 1 / 48 / 96 / 1,152**.
  No source overflow, expansion omission, target rejection, trail eviction or truncation.
  Every simulation sample has zero checkpoint-capture bytes; 716 event readback samples.
  Estimated effect buffers 540,580 bytes, not total device memory. Peak observed live count
  690 is concurrent occupancy, not total births. Aggregate simulation p50/p95/p99
  0.885/1.642/1.806 ms includes idle tail and asynchronous phase sampling: no live-finale
  performance certification or speed comparison with multi-break.
- `f5c-crackle-host-cues.json`: actual Bevy messages, no independent sound clock. Epochs 1/2/3.
  Initial and restarted streams each contain one launch at tick 1, one main break at tick 80,
  and 27 crackle packets / 96 carrier deaths across ticks 134–164. Seek to frame 160 reconstructs
  through tick 159, then delivers all five remaining packets / eight carrier deaths, ticks
  160–164. Reconstruction is silent; resumed/restarted kinds, positions, ticks and counts
  match the original stream. Zero stale messages happened in this run; delayed old readback
  rejection remains covered by the deterministic F5B tests.
- `f5c-crackle-review/review-manifest.json`: close/audience/wide × eight frames = 24 PNGs,
  base revision `4a4abbf0` with dirty source hashes recorded. Cohort/backend/response/cleanup
  checks pass. Close captures observe main peak 96, carrier peak 85 and spark peak 624;
  staggered short lifetimes mean these peaks need not reach 96/1,152. Endpoint live particles,
  occupied/retired histories, evictions and truncation are measured zero at frame 420.
  Inspected contact sheets show red expansion followed by warm brief dots and lingering smoke.
  Sparse distant pops need moving-footage/reference review; no artistic or golden approval.
